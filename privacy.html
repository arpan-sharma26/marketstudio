How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law, and then delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email hello.marketstudio@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.
